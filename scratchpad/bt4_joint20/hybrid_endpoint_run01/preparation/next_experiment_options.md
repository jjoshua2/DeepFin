# Options after the completed H20 package

Read-only preparation, September 7. No next arm is selected or queued here; H20 outcomes were not read. Recommendation is conditional on its completed C100/G100/C400 package.

Pure BT4 policy is the most distinct next endpoint among the existing profiles. It tests whether removing the SF policy anchor can help, including at higher search budget. It does not test a wholly LC0-trained network: SF-derived value and auxiliary targets stay unchanged. Keep the already designed 400-simulation probe if this option is selected, even after a valid shallow loss; a one-epoch loss would only reject that tested horizon.

G50 is the lowest-preparation-cost alternative, with a published 18,910,484-row corpus and reviewed first-shard/membership evidence. It tests a coarse higher global dose, not the same intervention as H20. If H20 beats G20 but loses to C, that indicates a benefit from its near-good redistribution at equal ideal outside mass while the added tail has not beaten C. G50 then has lower priority than the pure endpoint or a different SF target mechanism; that is not evidence that all larger doses fail. If H20 beats C, G50 is a reasonable coarse follow-up to distinguish whether broader BT4 use works without preserving C's conditional ranking. Neither branch forces a queue.

Longer training is the most important readiness investment if the goal is transferring a winning family to a much larger bootstrap. Current one-epoch comparisons cannot distinguish a worse asymptotic target from a target learned more slowly. Test two substantively different competitive families at equal two-epoch exposure and matched initialization/schedules, rather than extending only the apparent winner and comparing it to a one-epoch C. Record epoch-one and epoch-two checkpoints prospectively. A second-epoch continuation is acceptable only if optimizer/scaler/global-step/RNG state and the intended learning-rate trajectory are preserved and verified; otherwise use matched training from scratch. Changing the objective at the same time does not isolate the horizon question.

This is not currently a flag-only operation. `lc0_control_train.py:2100` accepts only the exact one-shot sampler batch count, and rejects both shorter and longer requests. Its CLI has no implemented resume/epochs entry point. The current 88-step release cycle is derived per train_steps call; epoch-boundary behavior and partial windows must be deliberate. The fixed one-epoch launcher also checks exactly 36,935 batches/420 windows. A new multi-epoch sampler/receipt and launcher budget contract need focused qualification. Expected two-arm/two-epoch training is roughly 10–11 GPU hours based on completed epochs, plus chosen arenas and operational margin; this is an estimate, not a reserved allocation.

Raw-cp soft SF is a substantive separate hypothesis: recover graded SF ordering that the saturated WDL-to-Q transform can hide. The current deriver computes softmax(q/temp), not softmax(cp/temp); increasing --temp is not the proposed raw-cp experiment. Reuse retained complete legal-action d9 observations, preserve SF value labels and other fields, and qualify move/history/source alignment. The top-eight rank sidecar alone cannot construct a full legal policy. Entropy matching on training-only data is a rough dose control, not a mechanism match: C's mean entropy is 0.51547 nats versus source SF 0.56897, while G50 is 1.15572; C still has 50.06% of rows with top probability at least .99 versus G50's 12.14%. Match/report saturation strata and support as well as mean entropy. The BT4 sidecar's reported entropy is not automatically the entropy of its sharpened target. Avoid treating the 128-row feasibility sample as a full-corpus entropy survey.

Practical readiness: G50 needs a fresh final corpus qualification/schedule/manifest but no new target materialization. B100 has reviewed mixer and C100/C400 launcher profiles; its fixed output is not yet prepared/qualified. Raw-cp SF and multi-epoch training both need implementation. Preparing those contracts while H20 runs is useful; committing their compute before H20's final readout is unnecessary. A win from more search at a fixed trained checkpoint and a win from more training are distinct interactions, and single-seed opening intervals do not measure training-seed variance.

Scope: inspected source and existing summaries/reviews only; no payload scans, tests, inference, live-output reads or runtime edits. Historical control/purity limitations remain. Existing completed C > G20 and fresh C > E0T05 results are context, not a proof of target optimality.
