## Deciding technical rule and controls

Success requires complete original row/game order and denominators, authentic
read-only source lineage, exact original vector/value bytes and declared
shapes/dtypes, correct current-P0 context/POV and explicit masks/statuses. A second
owned validator must freshly reopen original source and the complete candidate,
recompute its expected observations and match every identity, payload, inventory
and count. Both phases must close PASS with all owned births closed. Independent
closed evidence review and root acceptance follow; producer/validator child
statuses remain **CANDIDATE**, not ROOT_ACCEPTED or training admission.

Refuse structural/history/source/role/hash/clock/POV/move-map/duplicate/custody,
unsafe codec/header, output overlap, size or owner/admission failures. Existing
invalid label classifications remain retained with false masks; they do not
become runtime evidence of a bad search or guessed corrected targets. There is
no coverage threshold, fit metric, confidence interval, training mask or strength
gate. Wire identity, bounds/full PV and per-candidate effort remain unknown where
unpreserved; passing narrower source-data checks does not recover them.

Focused synthetic tests must expose retained-ply gaps, wrong game/history/input
or mirror identity, flag/payload ambiguity, native-versus-stored POV semantics,
invalid/nojoin masks, policy byte changes, complete denominators, source and
candidate inventory mutation, unsafe NPY headers, allocation caps, exact phase
admission/closed-owner checks and durable late-failure markers. Tests are UNRUN;
source/AST review does not count as their runtime qualification.

Reuse the existing fictional four-row direct-CLI input for the first positive
producer/validator chain. Keep three source identities separate: its fictional
producer `1` repeated 40 times; the actual historical census execution `70db0b6f…`;
and the new committed sidecar source/runtime, to be pinned after review. The
fixture's stored WDL is **native-derived**, not CP-logistic. Its accepted history
and three joins/one nojoin do not cover invalid/missing branches or original-G1
CP-logistic behavior; focused unit controls and later fixed-G1 validation have
separate scopes. Do not edit the old fixture or pretend its fake documents are
real collection/outcome authority. Current-main `ext_check.py` has a separately
pinned allowlist-only delta; this is not a blanket old-runtime/new-source identity
