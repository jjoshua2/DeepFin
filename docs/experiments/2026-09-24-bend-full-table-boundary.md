## Source interface

The new Nat and U32 full-table contracts remove the previous per-key before/after
counts from callers. Every key observes the same symbolic full-table expression:
actual extras after actual 128-block table construction beginning at key 0 and
offset 512. Depth/count remain symbolic with equalities to 17/128 to avoid
normalizing the entire concrete allocation during elaboration. Occupancy and
seed are arbitrary; extras have their existing square budget. Complete final
array/value pairs are retained.

The exact prior FullDomain certificate supplies every selected-key decomposition.
`Binding.image` transports that decomposition to the uniform recipe. The final
U32 contract derives key conversion itself. This is consolidation of the existing
independent attack/storage result, not a new proof of unrelated chess legality.

The actual zero-argument `Tables.build` signature/body is linked by a fail-closed
token guard to the proof recipe. The guard checks every construction default,
not just a file hash or the existence of the function. It is intentionally
stricter than semantic equivalence and is labeled separately from source proofs.
**No checked closed Tables.build() equality is claimed.** The initial direct
reflexivity attempt timed out after 30 seconds; its exact source/log are retained.
