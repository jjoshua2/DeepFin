## Why both effective fractions equal one

`losses.py:2962–3094` first normalizes the configured SF/search weights and obtains `sf_available` and `search_available` from actual presence masks. Its effective row numerators are `sum(sf_available * keep)` and `sum(search_available)`, provided the corresponding label tensor exists. These are divided by all batch rows in `trainer.py:2051–2052`. Neither numerator is multiplied by its configured blend coefficient. Thus both diagnostics being 1 means both label sources are available on the sampled views without disagreement damping; it does not mean a 1:1 blend, a double-counted target, or nonzero SF supervision.

All40 recorded loss calls per arm show SF fraction0. FULLA search1 means outcome0; FULLB search.8 means verified outcome.2. Damping is0 and terminal-transfer disabled. Therefore the observed availability1 creates no fallback outcome mass: FULLA mainWDL is search alone; FULLB mainWDL remains .8search+.2verified outcome. Categorical is a separately verified rawQ scalar blend before HL-Gauss/F16, not inferred from these availability diagnostics.

Similarly positive `m_sf_policy_floor`, floor binding/mass diagnostics and `has_sf_p0_regret` do not prove a floor gradient: `losses.py:3372–3394` computes diagnostics even with zero objective weight. Actual floor w is0, as are SF own/own-regret/reply/eval/volatility terms. Their numerical presence is not actual optimization influence.

1. If both trained arms regress against the donor, ask whether a simpler own-policy-distillation control preserves the donor better than the common full loss bundle at the same delivered dose. A future prospective matched fresh-donor/data/draw/RNG comparison can hold the producer fixed and change only this declared objective-family contrast, preserving all imported heads (inactive is not reset). The deciding metric must remain complete fixed playing contrasts versus donor and full-control, with a separately declared technical/harm rule; loss fit is auxiliary. If the simpler control regresses similarly, the hypothesis that the added common value/auxiliary bundle is the main cause is not supported. This diagnoses a bundle, not a single head or optimal recipe.

2. If both regress, or one merely preserves strength without the registered B benefit, ask whether an external value anchor helps beyond completed-Q self-distillation. The simplest later contrast would change the mainWDL source only and leave categorical/other losses fixed, rather than another two-head bundle. Before choosing any share, determine whether the available P1 SF labels carry useful independent value information at actual fullhistory roots; masks and low supervised loss cannot answer that. A future controlled question can use source-qualified teacher/value diagnostics as support and complete paired playing versus its search-only control/donor as the deciding endpoint. If the anchor improves fit/teacher agreement but not play (or harms it), the proposed mechanism is falsified at that registered dose/source. No coefficient, teacher-effort escalation, borrowed HOLD selection or new compute is warranted before the current donor/context readouts close. If a current arm passes instead, preserve that result's exact target/source scope and test replication before explaining it as SF-anchor absence or optimal outcome mixing.

The two questions remain hypotheses, conditional on unfinished playing evidence. They neither alter current thresholds nor claim an optimal formula, a general Elo gain, or the long-term Rofchade/SF/Lc0 milestone.
