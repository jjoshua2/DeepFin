The preserved A39 receipt identifies the read-only copy of the epoch-two checkpoint in the 174,053,867-row accumulated run. The completed run's RESULT metadata identifies that exact source path, epoch2/optimizer step680276, no epoch3, and binds the retained config d5b00c… and union selection fd8704…. Those two small metadata identities and the recent raw-selection receipt were checked directly. No checkpoint, row payload or model was opened.

## What is known

The actual-run recipe is SF fraction0/search fraction1, policy_target_temp1, no categorical rebuild, batch-masked-mean normalization. These field names specify the consumer, not the identity of the teacher. Its selected dataset is a mixture: 107,808,845 ordinary prepared rows plus66,245,022 accumulated raw rows. The launch YAML's categorical .69/.31 fields do not prove the stored categorical target had been rebuilt in that proportion: rebuild_categorical_target isfalse and precomputed targets are consumed.

Both contracts say root_inference_no_search: float32 175-plane v2_threats/lc0_root_legacy_meta/history_rep_fix inputs, native compact1858 policy, float32 side-to-move WDL probabilities in win/draw/loss order. Root inference has no teacher search effort to infer: it is not a documented Stockfish node budget or MCTS policy target.

The ordinary selection adds explicitly described pieces:2,292,804 staged1d3 root-inference rows;4,363,327 other prepared BT4 rows with native root policy T0.5 and normalized native WDL;961,126 successor3 and191,588 successor4 rows with stored T0.5 policy and normalized-teacher WDL. This audit did not establish a universal teacher identity/search effort/target construction for that original100M or a single model hash for every remaining ordinary subset. Do not turn the convenient phrase 'stored neural-teacher values' into a claim that all174m rows used the same named network.

Likewise stored categorical construction for every legacy portion is unknown from these receipts alone.

## What remains unknown

The hashed teacher identities and input/output contracts are known for the named raw subsets. Their human/public model names, architecture/training pedigree and whether either should be called Lc0, Stockfish or another family are not established by this audit. LC01858 and LC0-history encoding names do not identify a teacher implementation; BT4 is a label in the local contracts, not evidence of a specific engine. This audit did not open teacher models, infer their strength, authenticate every old payload, reconstruct every per-file prediction, or measure earlier search/pretraining exposure. The original100M has known row/storage identity here but unresolved complete teacher/target lineage.

## Can current R7 annotations preserve that SL teacher?

Not exactly. Current own-policy/soft/future targets come from A39's64-simulation MCTS; current search_wdl combines completed-search Q with A39's raw draw probability. That is a changed on-policy target family, even when the loss columns retain SF0/search1. The earlier root teachers supplied stored native policy/value distributions, not these completed-Q targets.

Current R7's SF pool instead annotates P1 after the sampled NET move: fullhistory5000nodes/MultiPV8, side-to-move CP-logistic WDL flipped into the original record POV, dense opponent-reply distribution and sparse scores. Such a P1 reply teacher cannot reconstruct the hashed teachers' native P0 policy/WDL probabilities, policy temperature transform or legacy auxiliary coverage. Even a fresh P0 SF analysis would remain a new teacher/source intervention, not exact SL preservation. No SF share or effort is justified merely by the old loss names.

There is some P0 information already available without assuming new queries: finalize.py derives sparse current-position P0 regret from the immediately preceding record's P1 raw MultiPV when the record/history alignment exists. Actual TRAIN-view has_sf_p0_regret coverage was~.9843, while record_sf_p0_policy isfalse/has_sf_p0_frac0. That can support a separately specified sparse SF-near-best floor on its covered subset, but is not a complete neural-teacher policy distribution, nor proof that a weight.8 would recreate the donor's effective objective. First/missing/unaligned positions have no such guarantee; absent top8 moves are unknown, not measured negatives.

An exact teacher-preserving continuation on new positions would need the correct original teacher identity for each intended target family and its actual full-history native outputs plus the original target transforms/masks. If the question is instead a new P0 SF value or move teacher, it needs explicitly aligned current-position information: where the preceding P1 annotation is demonstrably the same P0/fullhistory it may be reusable; elsewhere actual new P0 annotation would be additional information. Neither can be supplied by relabeling an existing P1 WDL/reply field or by resetting inactive heads.

This is a lineage and information-boundary audit, not a new objective/parameter/compute recommendation. Current candidate/controls, fixed study thresholds and prospectivity remain untouched. No unfinished context/arena outcomes were read.
