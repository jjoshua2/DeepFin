# Full512 old-cohort selected versus dual label cost

**Readout, 2026-09-29.** On the frozen 12,288-row BT4-v9/Ceres-v8 old cohort, selected one-teacher labels saved **38.888684%** of mean complete-arm wall against dual BT4+Ceres labels. The preregistered cost screen required at least 15% saving, positive contrasts in both opposite-order pairs, exact target parity and clean teardown; all passed. This advances only to a larger representative Full512 cost pilot. It grants no source, target, corpus, training, or 500M throughput credit.

| Late pair, D2 → S2 | 61.113501 s | 100.770229 s | 39.353616% |
| Mean of two per recipe | **60.061667 s** | **98.282399 s** | **38.888684%** |

The same frozen stage, 385 selected and 770 dual calls per arm, and exact BT4/Ceres model hashes were used throughout. All eight teacher jobs ran serially on one GPU. Each teacher/arm paid one neutral warmup before its real calls; the eight warmups were discarded from target rows. Each arm wrote and reread only its own targets, including a standalone selected-plus-complementary reconstruction for each D arm. The independent postrun collector reopened all saved feeds and heads, reconstructed all six target arrays, checked cross-arm selected-prefix and D1/D2 dual parity, rehashed source/model/loader bytes, and verified the terminal, postcommit, claim and resource receipts. The earlier two-arm run06 remains a **failed** fixed-order screen: S-first 123.259512 seconds versus D-second 140.008151 seconds, or 11.962617% saving against the same stage, calls and model bytes. Its S-first BT4/Ceres first real inference calls took 48.788/14.981 seconds, versus 0.050/0.199 seconds in run05 S1 after charged neutral warmups. Run06's D wall also included reconstruction dependent on S artifacts; in run05 every D arm used its own outputs and common final readback was outside the arm endpoint. Those startup-sensitive calls and changed readback boundary explain why the two ratios differ descriptively. They do not isolate a causal warmup effect: host/cache state differed between runs, and run06 had only a small-receipt independent audit. Neither run06 wall belongs in the new ABBA mean, and its failed gate is not rescored.

The smallest useful next cost test is a preregistered, roughly **50,000-row** same-input ABBA on qualified, admitted-unique Full512 **whole-game** rows, drawn from a frozen production-weighted source/opening/phase sampling frame rather than recycling the old cohort. Keep current-route BT4/Ceres models, selected routing, batch geometry, neutral warmups, separate own-arm target readback, full-byte parity, and the same 15%-plus-two-positive-pairs decision rule. Freeze the actual row count, resource caps and sampling weights before launch; retain gross stage/readback and output/I/O accounting. If the production-weighted frame or strict row admission is unavailable, this remains a conditional source-mix screen, not a 500M forecast. Two ABBA pairs provide no reliable sampling interval or production-scale wall bound.

The existing 500M sensitivity's **10.954 ideal label-days already assumes one selected teacher per row** using historical per-teacher rates. The 38.888684% selected-versus-dual arm saving must **not** be subtracted from that subtotal again. This batch-32 old-cohort test omits production-weighted yield, contention, packing and a full training visit, so it cannot establish 500M label days or a 30-day pipeline.
