# E2 resource qualification: proposed, not authorized to execute

The synthetic wrapper is CPU-only and rejects real bank execution. Its 16 GiB
address-space, 120 CPU-second, 180 wall-second ceilings are containment limits,
not evidence that the full donor experiment fits. Neither free hardware nor the
old queue's ready count releases its drain/STOP or transfers custody.

## Proposed one-shot disposable resource pilot

- Exact donor: externally pinned retained baseline SHA256
  `a39f900c944477c4ecf7f56cf0e1a6dd9486b67e5c2abb00973254e4bb6749b0`.
  Architecture must be the complete hash-bound retained schema, not inferred.
- One fixed predeclared batch of two already qualified stored175 rows; exact row,
  tensor, target and source receipts must be filled and independently reviewed
  before launch. No adaptive row or checkpoint substitution.
- Strict weights-only loader, disposable CPU model, one full production forward
  and backward; **no optimizer construction or step**, no checkpoint writes,
  publication, candidate update, match or generation. Do not resume stopped work.
- Proposed upper limits: one Torch/native thread, CUDA hidden, 16 GiB address
  space, 120 CPU seconds, 180 wall seconds, 8 MiB outputs; parent owns child
  process group and reaps on all abnormal exits. Fresh canonical custody/control
  admission through an explicitly authorized new finite route is mandatory.
- Record whole-child CPU/wall/peak RSS, forward/backward spans, exact batch/weight
  identities, finite gradients and unchanged model state. Preserve failure without
  retry. No success assertion if any bound or identity gate fails.

A passing batch-two pilot does not qualify batch256 or 128 optimizer updates.
Activation memory, Aurora state, repeated load-time bank validation and optimizer
costs need separate measured bounds. No linear extrapolation is treated as a
launch guarantee. A GPU route requires its own reviewed implementation, actual
memory/precision checks and current custody; this CPU qualification grants none.

## Finite experiment gates

128 admitted natural games from at most160 observed attempts; exclusions and full
history/row crosswalk retained. First eligible start-ordinal selection, no outcome
or completion-timing selection. Load-time shard/target inventories and externally
pinned bank/candidate/observation receipts must still match. Freeze one64x256 row
trace shared by both arms; strict checkpoint/architecture binding, fresh matched
Aurora named groups, full objective, global120-cycle LR, RNG and no-augmentation
controls. Search-only versus80/20 genuine outcome is the only arm intervention.
TB execution receipts and genuine-terminal exclusions are independent gates.

Current evidence: real tiny-transformer/Aurora64x2 per arm passed synthetic tests,
including post-warmup release and actual applied-LR comparison. This is not a
full-donor, batch256, GPU, or scientific-strength result.
