## Question and prior result

Does limiting repeated training exposure to at most 40 updates per collection
produce a final eight-window model that improves over the preserved epoch-two
donor? The only learning-setting change from the qualified corrected eight-window
recipe is `train_steps: 128 → 40`, including its strict startup-contract mirror.

The [first experiment](2026-11-11-epoch2-rl-transition.md) is closed. Its selected
40-step candidate scored 315 wins / 371 draws / 314 losses in its sole 1,000-game
confirmation: score 0.5005, paired normal 95% interval
[0.4766922849944062, 0.5243077150055937]. It established no confirmed gain. Neither
that candidate nor the 872-step endpoint is adopted. Those results, the original
two-endpoint selection and its used holdout remain immutable; no old games are
pooled into this experiment or reused as its confirmation.

The completed longer trajectory showed greater fitting/exposure and an ineligible
screen. That motivates one bounded exposure question, not a demonstrated cap,
clipping, learning-rate, teacher or sampler remedy. This experiment has one training
realization, not replicated training trials. The unchanged configured base seed
20261111 and a fresh Ray trial identity yield different realized learner/worker
streams. Trajectories, replay age/composition and schedule phase can differ, so this
is not a matched-RNG causal estimate of the cap's effect. Evaluation intervals are
testwise; no global familywise claim across experiments is made.

## Interpretation and next design

The bounded cap did not produce an eligible endpoint in this realization. Finite
weights, lower fitting loss, complete target coverage and a working sequential
pipeline did not establish strength. The 100% auxiliary hard-clip rate and
nonuniform sampling remain measurements to explain, not proven causes or
prescriptions to change clipping, learning rate, teacher blends or sampling.

The earlier [completed training-dose diagnosis identity](evidence/2026-11-12-epoch2-rl-cap40/readout.json)
binds external artifact `completed-training-dose-diagnosis-v1/readout.md`.
Its mechanism limits remain relevant: soft and categorical losses share search
information, aggregate loss reductions do not measure gradient contribution or
playing strength, holdout drift was unmeasured, and clipping telemetry excludes
Aurora matrix updates. Its prospective cap-40 suggestion was a hypothesis, not a
promise; this new experiment records the failed screen rather than rewriting that
prior proposal.

A later research design should isolate one falsifiable mechanism and include an
actual held-out behavior/target or update measurement before extending on-policy
dose again. Match the relevant source, starting state, replay and RNG where the
causal question requires it, and declare fresh unseen evaluation, uncertainty and
bounds prospectively. Banked observations can narrow that question; these results
do not select a guaranteed hyperparameter remedy. This is a design recommendation
only: no new code, training, model probes or evaluation is started here.

The longer-term Rofchade → Stockfish/Lc0 research direction remains an unmet
ambition. This record closes one bounded donor-relative exposure experiment; it
does not claim that broader strength goal is complete.
