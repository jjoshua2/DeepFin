## Candidate and deciding rule

New sources live only under native/bend_engine/standalone/proofs/forward_attacks.
They define an independent source scan, lift qualified pointwise leaper/slider
reversals through first-order limb reconstruction, retain complete returned arrays,
and substitute forward witnesses into all three castling safety stages.

The full importing consumer must request all four complete equations with no
assumed masks, lookup answers or legal-generator outputs. The helper/fixtures,
all five specific semantic controls, full consumer and final source/compiler
identity checks must pass together. A partial pass is not full qualification.
No existing runtime/proof/compiler source, pin, Base or workflow is altered.

## Resource and failure contract

GitHub Actions only; no local executor, GPU or live process. Whole qualifier:
1,600 seconds, two CPU cores of quota, 6 GiB RAM and no swap. Core helper180,
five controls60 each, full consumer1,050. These are test bounds, not theorem premises.

The five mutations reverse forward pawn color, omit the upper source limb,
drop queen diagonal witnesses, ignore strict-interior blockers and corrupt the
actual reverse-pawn query route. Only intended semantic rejection at the registered
definition counts. Parser, kind, quantity, ownership, missing-body, timeout and
memory failures leave NOT_COMPLETED. Reports are invalidated before each attempt,
and bind complete transitive sources plus compiler identity.

Old separate consumer timings (leaper29s, slider92s, castling579–763s) motivate
this bounded trial, but do not establish new timing. Do not raise the total bound
or modify trust assumptions automatically after failure.
