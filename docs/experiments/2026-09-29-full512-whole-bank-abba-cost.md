# Full512 whole-bank selected versus dual label cost

**Readout, 2026-09-29; independent full-byte audit passed.** On the frozen 58,820-row, 512-whole-game BT4-v9/Ceres-v8 bank, selected one-teacher labels saved **43.936202%** of mean complete-arm wall against dual BT4+Ceres labels in a serial S1 → D1 → D2 → S2 screen. The preregistered threshold was 15%, with positive savings in both opposite-order pairs, exact target parity, and clean teardown. The operator and independent full-byte collector both passed those checks. The result grants no source, target, corpus, training, or 500M throughput credit.

| Serial pair | Selected complete wall | Dual complete wall | Selected saving |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Early, S1 → D1 | 251.697610 s | 449.829908 s | 44.046048% |
| Late, D2 → S2 | 256.269416 s | 456.221847 s | 43.827895% |
| Mean of two per recipe | **253.983513 s** | **453.025877 s** | **43.936202%** |

All four arms used the same frozen 58,820 staged rows, source-route selection, model bytes, batch-32 geometry, and one neutral warmup per teacher and arm. Each S arm made 1,839 real calls; each D arm made 3,678. The selected-prefix targets and the independently reconstructed dual targets have matching cross-arm SHA-256 values in the operator readback. The experiment used one GPU and serial jobs.

The deciding denominator is each **complete arm wall**, including model/session setup, warmup, input conversion, inference, saved feed/head writes, compact target reconstruction, target write/readback, and arm-local forensic decode. For S1/S2 respectively, feed conversion took 28.838/29.383 seconds, host neural inference 76.738/80.800 seconds, compact target conversion 9.912/10.217 seconds, and compact target write/readback 0.315/0.319 seconds. For D1/D2 those same phases took 58.899/58.518, 154.430/164.341, 30.743/31.072, and 0.617/0.635 seconds. Arm-local forensic feed/head decode was a substantial 119.837/121.006 seconds for S and 181.422/182.133 seconds for D. Saved feed/head writes and their checks are likewise diagnostic work; these numbers must not be mistaken for the final sparse production-label writer's cost.

The 2,129.953-second total run also includes 14.838 seconds of shared staging and 695.715 seconds of final full-byte readback outside the four arm walls. The postcommit parent observed 141,761,127,475 logical `rchar+wchar` bytes, below the fixed 160 GiB observed cap; the full-wall limit was 7,200 seconds and the output-tree limit 16 GiB. These are observed refusal limits, not hard resource quotas. The operator terminal SHA-256 is `90d6f7a20c2c139f3b277e6a128621d5e9288d5b6cf4369519dae293bef0ac0c`, with a matching postcommit receipt. The independent collector rehashed all 51 output files (12,572,216,972 bytes), inputs and source pins, replayed the four arms and target parity, and passed in 690.978 seconds. Its compact receipt SHA-256 is `5fd373baf8298ab2baddbac46925703db4463196cfbeffe40e40a709587ac35c`.

The earlier 12,288-row old-cohort four-arm screen saved 38.888684%; its fixed input, output, and forensic costs differ from this bank, so the two percentages are descriptive and should not be pooled. The old two-arm run06 failed its fixed-order gate and is not rescored. The present bank is already source-crosslinked to 512 strict whole games, but it is still a conditional source-mix cost frame, not an admitted production-weighted 500M stream. The existing **10.954 ideal 500M label-days already assumes selected teachers**; this 43.936202% selected-versus-dual saving must not be subtracted from it again. It cannot establish 500M label days or a 30-day pipeline.

The next practical steps are a batch128 BT4/batch512 Ceres numerical-profile screen on the same bank, a sampled generating-owner raw-head reuse comparison, and a compact production target writer and readback.
